Reporting Technical Issues

Since WeConvertFiles operates locally in your browser, technical issues are often tied to specific client environments. When reporting a bug, please include:

  • The specific tool you were using (e.g., Sign PDF, Scale Image).
  • Your operating system (e.g., Windows, macOS, Android) and web browser version (e.g., Chrome, Safari, Firefox).
  • A brief description of what happened (e.g., "The browser froze during 3x Lanczos scale processing").
  • Whether the file failed to load, failed during conversion, or failed to download.

File Privacy Warning

To protect your privacy, do not attach confidential, private, or sensitive documents to email messages. If we need to investigate a file-specific parsing error, we will guide you on how to create a sanitized mock file.
