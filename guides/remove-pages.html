Remove Pages

100% Client-Side

Remove Pages allows you to delete unwanted slides or pages from a PDF. By entering page indexes to remove, the local parser builds a new page array, skipping the matching page elements, and outputs a cleaned document instantly.

Open Remove Pages

How It Works

1

Select PDF

Choose the PDF document that contains pages you want to delete.

2

Enter Page Indexes

Specify page numbers or ranges to remove (e.g. 2, 4-6) in the input block.

3

Remove & Save

Click Remove Pages to strip the selected slides in-memory and download the file.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I remove ranges and individual pages at the same time?

Yes, you can input a combination of ranges and page numbers, such as 1, 3, 5-7.

Is it possible to recover deleted pages?

No, once the cleaned file is compiled, those pages are gone. Make sure to keep a backup of the original.

Are my files safe during removal?

Yes. The document is parsed and cleaned in browser memory, meaning no server receives your data.
