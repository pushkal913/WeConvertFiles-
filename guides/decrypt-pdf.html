Frequently Asked Questions

Can I unlock a PDF without knowing the password?

No. For security and compliance, you must provide the correct password to unlock the document.

Will this tool remove printing and copying restrictions?

Yes, once unlocked, all permission restrictions regarding printing and copying text are removed.

Is my password safe?

Yes, the password is processed in memory on your device and is never transmitted over the network.
