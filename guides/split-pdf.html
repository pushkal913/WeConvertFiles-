Split PDF

100% Client-Side

Split PDF separates a single multi-page PDF document into individual single-page PDF files or extracts specific ranges into a ZIP folder. It executes locally in your browser using JavaScript and pdf-lib. The binary structure of each page is isolated, packed into a new PDF header, and zipped client-side.

Open Split PDF

How It Works

1

Upload PDF

Select the multi-page PDF document you wish to split or extract pages from.

2

Choose Option

Select whether you want to split all pages or extract specific page groupings/ranges.

3

Download ZIP

Click Split PDF to process your pages locally in memory. They will download bundled in a ZIP.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I split a PDF into custom page ranges?

Yes. You can choose to split every page individually or extract specific page groupings like page 1 to 5.

Is there a file size limit for splitting PDFs?

Since processing happens locally, it depends on your device memory. Files up to 100MB process in seconds.

Are my documents safe when using this tool?

Yes. All extraction operations occur strictly in your local browser sandbox. Your files are never uploaded.
