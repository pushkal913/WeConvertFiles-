100% Client-Side

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between PNG and JPG output?

JPG images use lossy compression, which yields much smaller file sizes than PNGs, making them ideal for emails.

Are my document images stored anywhere?

No. The conversion runs strictly in-memory. The files are immediately garbage collected after download.

Does it support password-protected PDFs?

No. You must unlock password-protected files using our Unlock PDF tool before converting them.
