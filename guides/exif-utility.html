EXIF Viewer & Remover

100% Client-Side

EXIF Viewer / Remover displays camera metadata stored inside JPEG, JPG, and PNG images, such as location coordinate maps, capture device names, and shutter apertures. To remove EXIF, the tool draws the raw visual pixel grid onto a clean canvas buffer and exports the image as a new blob, completely stripping all original metadata headers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What metadata gets removed?

All camera settings, GPS coordinate history, orientation fields, and device details are stripped. Only the visual pixel graphic is saved.

Why is it important for privacy?

When you share photos online, they can contain coordinates showing where the photo was taken. Stripping EXIF blocks geolocation tracking.
