Images to PDF

100% Client-Side

Images to PDF compiles PNG, JPG, or WebP images into a single clean PDF document. It runs locally using pdf-lib. The engine scales each image to fit standard page dimensions, adds a new page, and compiles them in sequence. This is perfect for archiving photos, documents, or screenshots.

Frequently Asked Questions

What image formats are supported?

WeConvertFiles supports PNG, JPEG/JPG, and WebP formats. You can mix and match files in the same queue.

Does this tool compress my images?

No. The images are embedded in their original resolution, ensuring the output PDF preserves maximum graphic detail.

Is there a limit on image count?

There is no hard limit, though large batches of high-resolution images may consume more browser memory.
