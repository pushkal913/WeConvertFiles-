How to Convert HEIC Photos to JPG or PNG

100% Client-Side

HEIC to JPG / PNG converts Apple HEIC photos to standard JPG or PNG images. Using the heic2any rendering package in parallel web worker buffers, the converter decodes the HEIF container and prints standard graphic pixels to a downloadable canvas format, making iPhone photos instantly compatible with Windows systems.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert multiple HEIC files?

Yes, you can upload multiple HEIC images. If multiple files are converted, they are compressed and downloaded as a ZIP folder.

Are my photos uploaded to any servers?

No. All image conversions happen locally in your web browser tab without any remote network uploads, keeping your photos secure.
