Organize PDF

100% Client-Side

Organize PDF lets you rearrange the page structure of your document. By entering a custom comma-separated page sequence, the engine rebuilds the page tree nodes locally, letting you reorder or duplicate pages instantly without uploading files to remote systems.

Open Organize PDF

How It Works

1

Upload PDF

Choose the PDF file you wish to reorder or reorganize.

2

Define New Order

Type the pages in your desired sequence (e.g. 3, 1, 2, 2 to repeat page 2).

3

Reorder & Download

Click Organize PDF to generate the newly structured document instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I duplicate pages using this tool?

Yes. If you repeat a page number in your sequence (like 1, 2, 2, 3), page 2 will be duplicated in the output.

What happens if I make a typo in the page numbers?

The tool checks your inputs. Invalid page numbers will be flagged, preventing errors.

Is it safe to organize bank statements here?

Yes. All rearrangement calculations occur locally inside your browser tab, ensuring absolute data security.
