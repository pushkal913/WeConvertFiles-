About WeConvertFiles

WeConvertFiles was founded with a single core mission: to make daily document processing fast, free, and privacy-focused. Unlike traditional online tools that require file contents to be uploaded for conversion, the supported WeConvertFiles tools process selected files directly on your device.

Local Client-Side Processing

By utilizing state-of-the-art WebAssembly compiles and modern Client-Side JavaScript libraries (such as pdf-lib.js, pdf.js, jsPDF, and SheetJS), all file manipulation happens in-memory inside your browser tab. When you select, sign, or scale files, the logic runs entirely on your device. Your data never leaves your computer, laptop, or mobile screen.

Our Monetization Model

To keep this service 100% free for students, freelancers, and businesses without asking for subscriptions, user accounts, or credit card inputs, we rely on digital advertising revenue. Ads allow us to cover our domain registrations, server hosting fees, and ongoing maintenance. If you find our tools useful, please consider whitelisting us on your AdBlocker extension so we can keep building free services.

Core Features Available

  • PDF Operations: Merge documents, split pages, extract ranges, rotate angles, and stamp watermarks.
  • Lightweight Approvals: Signature tools allowing you to draw or type signatures locally.
  • High Resolution Image Scaling: Local Lanczos-3, Bilinear, and Nearest-Neighbor interpolation algorithms.
  • Data Conversion: Convert CSV tables directly into clean JSON arrays or Excel documents.
