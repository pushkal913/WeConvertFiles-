Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

At WeConvertFiles, user privacy is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy details how we handle files, cookies, and ads when you access our tools.

1. Document and File Privacy

The core feature set of WeConvertFiles operates fully client-side. When you load files into tools (such as Merge PDF, Unlock PDF, or Scale Image), the processing is completed locally inside your web browser using HTML5 features, JavaScript libraries, and WebAssembly. Your files are never uploaded to our servers.

For cloud-based conversions like PDF to Word or Excel (which require server processing), files are transferred securely over HTTPS to conversion nodes and are automatically, permanently deleted from those systems immediately after conversion. We do not store or inspect your content.

2. Cookies and Data Analytics

To provide a reliable experience, we use basic browser LocalStorage to save interface preferences (such as dark mode selections). We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII). We may use privacy-compliant web analytics tools to monitor general site traffic patterns and aggregate browser data.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We partner with third-party networks, including Google AdSense, to serve ads on WeConvertFiles. Google uses cookies and device identifiers to serve personalized ads based on your previous visits to our platform or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to our site and other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting the AboutAds Info Portal.

4. Third-Party Websites and Services

WeConvertFiles contains links to external pages, services, or resource CDNs (like Cloudflare, Google Fonts). This Privacy Policy applies solely to WeConvertFiles. We recommend reviewing the policies of external platforms you visit.

5. Privacy Updates

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time as our platform features expand. Updates will be posted on this page with the modified "Last updated" date.
