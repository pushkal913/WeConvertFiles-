# WeConvertFiles Launch Checklist

## Replace before publishing

- Replace every `https://your-domain.com` value in HTML files, `robots.txt`, and `sitemap.xml` with your real domain.
- Confirm the support email `techknogeeks@zohomail.in` is the address you want visitors and ad reviewers to use.
- Re-upload the entire `outputs` folder to Netlify, not only `index.html`.

## Domain setup

- Buy the domain.
- Add it in Netlify under Domain management.
- Follow Netlify's DNS instructions.
- Make sure HTTPS is active.

## SEO setup

- Create a Google Search Console property for the domain.
- Verify ownership using Netlify DNS or an HTML file.
- Submit `https://your-domain.com/sitemap.xml`.
- Check that all important pages are indexed.

## Ad setup

- Apply for Google AdSense after the custom domain, contact page, privacy policy, and content pages are live.
- Add the AdSense script only after approval.
- Keep ads clearly separated from upload, convert, and download controls.

## Traffic plan

- Publish more focused pages for each real tool you add.
- Add comparison and how-to articles only when they genuinely help users.
- Avoid buying paid traffic until organic traffic and ad revenue are measurable.
